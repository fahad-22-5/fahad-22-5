### 🚀 About Me

A while back, I was bitten by a coding bug — and today, I build and scale mission-critical backend systems that move physical goods across continents.

I am a **Software Engineer** specializing in **high-throughput backend sortation engines**, **distributed message queues**, and **hardware-software integration (PLC / Put-to-Light / Robotics)**. 

- ⚡ **100K+ Parcels/Day**: Architect core sortation microservices in **.NET 8 / C#** sustaining peak loads of **10,000+ parcels/hour** for Tier-1 supply chains and quick-commerce operators.
- ⚙️ **Hardware-to-Software Bridge**: Engineer low-latency **PLC communication layers** that directly interface software logic with automated conveyor belts, diverters, and sensors on the warehouse floor.
- 💡 **End-to-End PTL Systems**: Delivered digital pick-to-light (PTL) workflows with LED visual guidance for India's largest retail and FMCG enterprises.
- 🌍 **International Deployments**:
  - 🇪🇸 **Madrid, Spain**: 2.5 months on-site managing cross-border sortation systems, automated logistics, and last-mile dispatch for a major European e-commerce hub.
  - 🇸🇦 **Riyadh, Saudi Arabia**: Deployed and remotely tested high-throughput PTL and conveyor automation for a leading Middle Eastern apparel fulfillment center.
- 🎓 **3-Continent Education**: Computer Science & Engineering graduate (Amity University) with international study-abroad terms at **Birkbeck, University of London (UK)** & **Adelphi University (New York, USA)**.
- 📄 **Published Researcher**: 2 peer-reviewed research papers published in **IEEE** (Computer Vision & CNN for Sign Language) and **Wiley HBET** (AI-driven CBT Mental Health Gamification).
